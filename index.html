UIU Tuition & Installment Guide (User Manual)

Official guidelines, installment rules, waiver formulas, and sample student scenarios.

📌 Overview & Programs

This calculator is specifically built for United International University (UIU) students to accurately compute tuition fees, official installment milestone splits, waiver/scholarship deductions, actual payment adjustments, and calendar drop refund credits.

💡 Admission Batch Tuition Rates & Settings:

UIU tuition fee rates depend on your admission year. Currently configured rate: ৳ 5,525.00 / credit.

• Spring 2024 & Newer Batches: ৳ 6,500.00 / credit

• Older Batches: ৳ 5,525.00 / credit (or ৳ 5,000.00 / credit for earlier batches)

👉 You can change this rate anytime in the ⚙ Settings modal to match your batch!

⚙ Settings & Batch Customization Guide

Not every student has the same tuition rate or department fees. UIU charges different rates depending on admission year and academic program. All of these can be fully customized by clicking the ⚙ Settings icon at the top right of the page.

1. Per-Credit Tuition Rate (৳) Batch-Dependent

• Spring 2024 to Present Batches: Students admitted recently pay ৳ 6,500.00 per credit.

• Fall 2022 to Fall 2023 Batches: ৳ 5,525.00 per credit.

• Earlier Batches: ৳ 5,000.00 per credit or lower.

Where to find: Settings > Core Rates & Fees > Per-credit rate.

2. Trimester / Semester Fee (৳) Standard: ৳ 6,500

The fixed institutional fee charged each academic term. Standard undergraduate trimesters charge ৳ 6,500.00, while 6-month B.Pharm semesters charge ৳ 9,750.00.

Where to find: Settings > Core Rates & Fees > Trimester / Semester fee.

3. Mandatory Lab Fee (৳) B.Pharm: ৳ 5,000

Certain departments like Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) require a fixed separate lab fee of ৳ 5,000.00 per semester. For CSE, EEE, and BBA, set this to 0.

Where to find: Settings > Core Rates & Fees > Mandatory Lab fee.

4. Retake Policy Rates (%) 50% / 100%

Under official UIU policy, 1st-time retakes receive a 50% discount (you pay 50% tuition). Subsequent repeats (2nd+ retake) pay 100% full tuition.

Where to find: Settings > Core Rates & Fees > Retake rates.

5. Minimum Fresh Credits for Waiver Default: 9 Credits

UIU requires students to take at least 9 fresh credits in a trimester to remain eligible for scholarship or waiver discounts. Taking fewer credits automatically suspends the waiver for that trimester.

Where to find: Settings > Scholarship & Waiver Policy > Minimum fresh credits.

6. Default Deposit at Registration (৳) Standard: ৳ 20,000

UIU asks students to pay an advance deposit of ৳ 20,000.00 during registration, and then splits the remaining balance across installments. Set to 0 if you do not pay at registration.

Where to find: Settings > Scholarship & Waiver Policy > Default deposit.

7. Campus Services & Late Fines Custom Slabs

Configure defaults for University Bus (৳ 8,000), Gymnasium (৳ 2,000), Late Installment Fine (৳ 500 per installment), and Late Registration Fine (৳ 1,000).

Where to find: Settings > Campus Services & Late Fines.

8. Installment Counts & Milestone Dates Full Flexibility

Configure 3 installments (40%, 30%, 30%) or 4 installments (25% each), plus customize official UIU calendar deadlines for 100% drop, 50% withdrawal, and milestone due dates.

Where to find: Settings > Milestone Dates & Installment Count.

🎓 Course Categories & Billing Rates

1. Fresh / Regular Credits Payable: 100% (Eligible for Waiver)

Standard courses taken for the first time. Eligible for merit scholarships and tuition fee waivers provided the minimum 9 fresh credit threshold is satisfied.

2. Non-Waiver Credits (FYDP, Capstone, Practicum) Payable: 100% (No Waiver/Scholarship)

Special project or terminal courses such as Final Year Design Project (FYDP I, II, III), Capstone Project, or Internships. Under UIU academic policies, students must pay full credit fees regardless of scholarship/waiver standing.

3. Retake (First-Time) Payable: 50%

Under official UIU rules, retaking a course for the very first time qualifies for a 50% tuition concession. You only pay half the per-credit tuition rate.

4. Retake (Not First-Time) Payable: 100%

Courses being repeated for the second time or more (3rd attempt+) are charged at the full 100% tuition rate.

💎 Waiver & Scholarship Rules

Minimum Credit Rule: Undergraduate students must enroll in at least 9 fresh credits in the trimester to be eligible for scholarship or waiver discounts. If you take fewer than 9 fresh credits, the discount is suspended automatically.
How Waiver Discount is Deducted (UIU Rule) Cuts from 1st Installment First

UIU splits gross fees into nominal installments (40%, 30%, 30%), and then deducts your waiver/scholarship discount directly from the 1st installment!

• If discount ≤ 1st installment: 1st installment is reduced, while 2nd and 3rd installments remain standard nominal amounts.

• If discount > 1st installment (e.g. 50% or 100% waiver): The 1st installment becomes ৳ 0 (skipped), and the remaining discount cascades into the 2nd installment.

📅 Installment Milestones & Payment Tracker

UIU requires students to meet cumulative payment thresholds by official calendar deadlines:

• Deposit at Registration: As part of tuition fees, students typically pay ৳ 20,000 at registration. The remaining balance is split across installments. (If no deposit is paid, the entire bill is split).

• 1st Installment (40%): Payable by official 1st installment milestone deadline (Cumulative requirement = 40%).

• 2nd Installment (30%): Payable by official 2nd installment milestone deadline (Cumulative requirement = 70%).

• 3rd Installment (30%): Payable by official 3rd installment milestone deadline (Cumulative requirement = 100%).

Payment Tracker & Cumulative Adjustments:

When you enter the amount you actually paid in each installment row:

• Overpayment: If you pay more than scheduled (e.g., paid ৳ 15,000 when ৳ 12,350 was due), the ৳ 2,650 surplus automatically reduces your 2nd installment!

• Underpayment: If you pay less than scheduled, the remaining shortfall carries forward into your next installment so that you always reach the 70% or 100% milestone required by the university.

🔄 Course Drop & Refund Policy

100% Adjustable Refund By Oct 31, 2026

Courses dropped within this window receive a 100% credit adjustment applied to your student ledger. Course is removed from transcript.

50% Refund ('W' Grade) Nov 01 – Nov 09, 2026

Withdrawal window. 50% credit adjustment is refunded to your account. A withdrawal grade ('W') appears on your grade sheet.

Dropped Today (Auto-Detect): Selecting this option automatically inspects today's actual calendar date against UIU official deadlines to apply the correct refund rate instantly.
